make_orbit_kpoint_irreps#

Package: libcasm-configuration v3.0a1

libcasm.configuration.make_orbit_kpoint_irreps(
supercell_kpoints: SupercellKpoints,
supercell_dof: SupercellDoF,
verbosity: str | None = None,
) → tuple[source]#

Make symmetry-adapted irrep decompositions for each k-point orbit.

For each k-point orbit, this function constructs a complex plane wave basis spanning all independent k-points in the orbit, then runs ComplexIrrepDecomposition using the factor group (without explicit translation matrices, since translations act as scalar phases in the complex basis and do not further split irreps). The result is equivalent to running IrrepDecomposition on the full supercell DoF space with the complete supercell symmetry group, but works orbit-by-orbit to keep the matrix dimensions small (at most (|orbit|/2) * n_dof_per_site complex DoF per orbit regardless of supercell volume).

The complex symmetry-adapted basis is converted to a real physical basis as follows. For TRIM orbits the imaginary parts of the modes vanish and the real part is used directly. For non-TRIM orbits, each complex mode v_j = B @ c_j (where B is the complex plane wave basis and c_j is a complex SAS column) contributes two real physical modes, sqrt(2)*Re(v_j) and -sqrt(2)*Im(v_j), which are orthonormal unit vectors.

Parameters:

  • supercell_kpoints (SupercellKpoints) – The SupercellKpoints instance containing k-point data.

  • supercell_dof (SupercellDoF) – The SupercellDoF instance containing DoF space data.

  • verbosity (Optional[str] = None) – Verbosity level for progress messages. Options are None, "none", "standard", and "verbose".

Returns:

  • kpoint_irreps (list[libcasm.irreps.IrrepInfo]) – One IrrepInfo per irreducible subspace found across all k-point orbits.

  • dof_space (libcasm.clexulator.DoFSpace) – Symmetry-adapted DoF space whose basis columns are the real-valued symmetry-adapted modes ordered by orbit then irrep.

  • axis_irrep_info (list[AxisIrrepInfo]) – One AxisIrrepInfo per column of the symmetry-adapted basis. The kpoint_index field lists all k-point indices belonging to the orbit that contributed to the axis.
